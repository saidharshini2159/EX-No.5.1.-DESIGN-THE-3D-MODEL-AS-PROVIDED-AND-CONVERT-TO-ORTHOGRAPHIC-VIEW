# EX No.5.1.DESIGN THE 3D MODEL AS PROVIDED AND CONVERT TO ORTHOGRAPHIC VIEW
## DATE:11-11-2026

## AIM: 
To create orthographic view of given 3D model

## REQUIREMENTS: 
1. Autodesk fusion 360
2. Windows 10
3. 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 1 GB or more)
4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 

## PROCEDURE:

1. Create the Base Flange: Sketch the rounded, diamond-like base profile on the top plane and extrude it upward to form the bottom plate.
2. <img width="657" height="627" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-16 015836" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c736928a-d81c-4e6c-9e94-b5bcc9a6bde4" />


3. Extrude the Center Hub: Sketch two concentric circles at the origin and extrude them up to create the main hollow cylindrical boss.
4. <img width="665" height="711" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-16 020756" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f8eeab89-f357-4953-92d1-4bc30237d8d3" />

5. Add the Side Wings: Sketch the two opposing triangular side extensions from the center hub and extrude them to a medium height.
6. Create the Front & Back Bosses: Sketch and extrude the two smaller, lower rounded tabs extending perpendicular to the main wings.
7. Add Support Ribs: Sketch triangular profiles on the side planes connecting the top of the side wings down to the base plate, then extrude or use the Rib tool.
8. <img width="658" height="733" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-17 162119" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bc3835ae-e130-4e3d-9c40-429e6a51bc8b" />

9. Cut the Center Stepped Bore: Use the Extrude Cut or Revolve tool inside the center cylinder to create the internal stepped groove and bottom slot.
10. <img width="692" height="713" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-17 162347" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1b7c2c70-86ce-4e6a-8541-fed43638b99a" />

11. Cut the Mounting Holes: Use the Hole tool to place four counterbored holes on the base flange and two standard through-holes on the side wings.
12. <img width="727" height="813" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-17 162452" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2686f5f2-fb02-4b5a-a136-cd845a87e18c" />

13. Drill the Tab Holes: Add two smaller through-holes into the front and back perpendicular bosses.
14. Apply Fillets: Use the Fillet tool to round off all sharp vertical corners, base edges, and rib intersections for a smooth cast look.
15. <img width="703" height="646" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-17 162539" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/63d5dc3a-291f-48d9-91a5-2c89701b7751" />

16. Add Chamfers: Apply a slight Chamfer to the top rim of the main center cylinder and the mounting holes to finish the part.
